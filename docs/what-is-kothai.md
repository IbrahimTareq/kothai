# What is Kothai?

Kothai is a self-hosted save-all manager. You drop links, paste text or save images, and it stores everything locally in a single SQLite file. It classifies your notes automatically and lets you search by meaning, not just keywords. Ask it a question and it pulls up the relevant cards from your collection to answer. One container, no cloud account, runs on your own hardware.

The AI part is optional. Turn it on and Kothai will extract metadata from links, caption your images, tag notes by topic and embed everything for semantic search. If you'd rather not run models locally you can point it at any LLM endpoint instead. Or turn AI off entirely and use it as a fast, private bookmark manager. Your data stays on your machine unless you explicitly choose to send inference elsewhere.
