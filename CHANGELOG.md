# Changelog

## [2.0.0](https://github.com/IbrahimTareq/kothai/compare/v1.4.0...v2.0.0) (2026-11-25)


### ⚠ BREAKING CHANGES

* on-device models are no longer shipped; --local is rejected.
* **ai:** on-device models are gone; the /api/models/files routes, residency settings, KOTHAI_MODELS_DIR and KOTHAI_AI_EMBED_PROVIDER are removed.
* POST /api/save refuses anything that is not a link, and the Telegram bot no longer saves text or photos.
